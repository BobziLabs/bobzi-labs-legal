Pocket Ricochet Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 November 2026 · Last updated: 27 November 2026

In short: Pocket Ricochet is a puzzle game by Bobzi Labs for players of all ages. To save your progress, the game uses a guest account; players aged 16 or older can also use an email or Google account. We use your information only to run the game. We do not sell it, show ads, offer real-money purchases or track you across other apps.

1. Who we are

This policy covers the Android game Pocket Ricochet (package com.bobzilabs.pocketricochet), developed by Bobzi Labs, Lebanon ("we", "us"). Bobzi Labs decides how this information is used. Privacy questions and requests: bobziLabs@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Your game account

  • Guest account. When you start playing, we create a guest account identified by a random user ID. No name, email or password is needed.
  • Email account (optional, age 16 or older). If you create or link an email account, we store your email address, whether it is verified, and your password. Our sign-in provider keeps the password in a protected (hashed) form, and we never see it.
  • Google account (optional, age 16 or older). If you choose "Continue with Google", Google confirms your identity to our sign-in provider and shares your email address. The sign-in provider may also store the name and profile picture that Google provides. The game does not display or use them.

Game data

  • Your player profile: your username, and your chosen avatar, frame, ball, cannon and theme. Usernames are not shown to other players.
  • Progress and statistics: levels played and completed, stars, best results, unlocked items and gameplay statistics.
  • Energy and Sparks: in-game balances and the records of how they were earned or spent. These are virtual items with no real-money value.
  • Level attempts and results, including the shot inputs needed to check that a result is genuine (anti-cheat).

Device and technical information

  • A random identifier created by the game when it is installed. We store it only in hashed form, and use it to keep your saves and usage limits consistent.
  • When the game connects to our servers, our hosting provider (Google) processes technical information such as your IP address and device type, as part of handling and securing the request.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your contacts, photos, files, messages, microphone or camera data, phone number, advertising ID, payment details, or location from your device. We do not use analytics or crash-reporting tools.

Information kept only on your device

The game keeps a local copy of your progress, settings, sign-in session and your answer to the age question in its private app storage on your device. The stored sign-in credential is encrypted where the device supports it. This copy is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

3. How we use information

  • To run the game, save your progress and, for email and Google accounts, let you recover it on another device.
  • To run the in-game economy (Energy, Sparks, daily gifts) and check level results to prevent cheating.
  • To let you sign in, verify your email address and reset your password.
  • To keep the service secure and reliable, for example by limiting abusive requests and fixing errors.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or build profiles for marketing. Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on providing the game you asked for, and on our legitimate interest in keeping it secure and fair.

4. Children and the age question

Pocket Ricochet is made for players of all ages, including children. Before any sign-in choice, the game asks every player the year they were born, without suggesting an answer, and asks them to confirm they have read this policy. The game keeps only the result ("16 or older" or "guest only") and when it was given, on your device; never the year of birth.

Players under 16, and anyone who has not answered, play the complete game with a guest account. Email accounts, Google sign-in, account linking and account switching are not offered to them, and usernames are picked from a list of fun names instead of typed. Once a year, a younger player can answer again. Age answers are self-declared, so they cannot be fully verified.

If a player who already has an email or Google account answers that they are under 16, the game turns off its account features. A parent or guardian can delete that account in the game, or ask us to delete it at bobziLabs@gmail.com (see Delete your Pocket Ricochet account).

For every player, we collect only what is needed to save progress and keep the game working: the random account and device identifiers and the game data described above. There are no ads, purchases, chat or other social features. If we learn that we hold contact information from a child under 16, we will delete it.

5. Advertising and in-app purchases

This version of Pocket Ricochet shows no advertising and offers no real-money purchases. It does not include advertising or billing software and does not access your device's advertising ID. If this changes, we will update this policy before releasing the change.

6. Service providers and sharing

We use these Google services to run the game. Google processes data on our behalf under its own terms:

  • Firebase Authentication: guest, email and Google sign-in.
  • Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions / Cloud Run: game servers and saved data.
  • Sign in with Google (Android Credential Manager): only if you choose it.
  • Google Play: app distribution.

Google's privacy information: Google Privacy Policy and Privacy and Security in Firebase. Data may be processed in countries other than yours. We do not share your information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

7. Security

All communication between the game and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Our servers check every request against your own account. Passwords are handled only by our sign-in provider. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. How long we keep data

DataHow long
Account, profile, progress, Energy and Sparks, attempt and transaction recordsUntil the account is deleted
Short-term request-limit records (abuse prevention)Removed when the account is deleted
Deletion record (see below)As long as needed to stop a deleted account being recreated
Server request logs kept by Google, which may include IP address and device typeA limited period under Google Cloud's logging settings. Our own server code does not write your account ID, email or game data to logs.
Emails about privacy or deletion requests, and our record of handling them12 months after the request is resolved
Data on your deviceUntil you uninstall the game or clear its data

Deletion record. When an account is deleted, we keep a small record so that the deleted account cannot be recreated, for example by another device that was still signed in. It contains no game data, username or email. It holds a one-way hash of the deleted account's ID, the deletion status and timestamps. The hash can still be matched with that account ID, so we treat it as personal data.

9. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and its game data at any time in the game (Settings → Account & privacy → Delete account) or by email. See Delete your Pocket Ricochet account for the steps, how email requests are verified, and what is deleted. Uninstalling the game does not delete your account.

10. Your choices and rights

You can play without an email or Google account, change your username and choices in the game, and ask for your account to be deleted at any time. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, and to object to or restrict its processing. To make a request, email bobziLabs@gmail.com from the address linked to your account. We will reply within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles data, we will update this page and its "Last updated" date before the change takes effect.
