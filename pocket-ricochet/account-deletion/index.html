Delete your Pocket Ricochet account

Pocket Ricochet (com.bobzilabs.pocketricochet) by Bobzi Labs · Last updated: 27 November 2026

You can ask us to delete your Pocket Ricochet account and its game data at any time. Deletion is permanent: your levels, stars, Energy, Sparks and profile cannot be restored afterwards.

In the game

Go to Settings → Account & privacy.

  • If Delete account is shown, tap it and then Delete forever. Email and Google accounts are asked to sign in again to confirm. Guest accounts are deleted directly, with no registration needed.
  • If Delete account is not shown yet, tap Account deletion help or Contact support and follow the email steps below.

By email

  1. Send an email to bobziLabs@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Pocket Ricochet account".
  2. Send it from the email address linked to your account. For a Google account, that is your Google email address. Say whether you sign in with email or with Google.
  3. We reply to that address and ask you to confirm the request from the same address. We act only on a confirmation that comes from that address and passes standard email authentication checks. We do not rely on the sender name alone. This is how we check that you own the account.
  4. We then delete your account and its game data, not just the sign-in.

We will never ask for your password, a verification code or any sign-in token. Do not send them to anyone.

We acknowledge requests within 7 days. We complete deletion within 30 days of your confirmation, unless the law requires otherwise. We email you when it is done.

Guest accounts

Guest accounts have no email or password, so we cannot confirm ownership of a guest account by email. A player ID alone is not proof, because anyone could copy it. Guest accounts hold no name, email or other contact details.

  • If you still have the game installed: delete the guest account in the game when Delete account is shown (see above).
  • If you no longer have the original installation (uninstalled, cleared data or lost device): nobody can access that guest account any more, and we cannot verify a request for it. The data stays on our servers but is not used.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (guest, email or Google sign-in) and email address.
  • Your profile: username, avatar, frame, ball, cannon and theme.
  • Your progress, stars, statistics and unlocked items.
  • Your Energy and Sparks balances and their records.
  • Level attempt and result records, the hashed device identifier and request-limit records.

What may be kept, and why

  • A deletion record with a one-way hash of your account ID, its status and timestamps. It contains no game data, username or email. It is kept so that a device that was still signed in cannot recreate the deleted account.
  • Server request logs kept by our hosting provider (Google), which may include IP address and device type, for a limited period under Google Cloud's logging settings. They are kept for security and reliability.
  • Our email exchange about your request, and a short internal record of it (dates and a one-way hash of the account ID, with no game data). These are kept as proof that we handled the request, and deleted 12 months after it is resolved.

Uninstalling the game removes the data on your device but does not delete your account on our servers.

Parents and guardians

You can delete your child's account in the game on your child's device, or email us from an address linked to the account.
